If you’re moving for work, university or to join family, start with the [Netherlands moving checklist](/guides/move-to-the-netherlands/). This guide covers steps that often depend on where you live, which passport you hold and where your documents were issued.

## Before you travel

1. **Check your residence route and entry visa.** If you hold a Nigerian passport, you usually need an MVV, the Dutch long-stay entry visa. Check the IND’s exemptions for your situation. If you need an MVV and the IND approval letter names Nigeria, follow the instructions for the Dutch consulate-general in Lagos. Use the location named in your letter. [IND MVV exemptions](https://ind.nl/en/mvv-exemptions) · [MVV sticker in Nigeria](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/mvv-long-stay/apply-nigeria)
2. **Prepare your civil documents early.** NetherlandsWorldwide says Nigerian documents must be legalised by Nigeria’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs. It explains which birth records the Netherlands accepts and what to bring if your birth was registered under an older system. You can submit these documents in English. If a certificate was issued elsewhere, including Ghana or Senegal, use the instructions for that issuing country. [Nigerian documents](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/legalisation/foreign-documents/nigeria) · [Legalisation by issuing country](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/legalisation/foreign-documents)
3. **Find an address where you can register.** Ask before paying a deposit. If you’re coming to study, contact your institution about housing as soon as you have an offer. [Find a home](/guides/find-a-home/) · [Study in NL housing guide](https://www.studyinnl.org/life-in-nl/finding-a-place-to-live)

## Inviting parents, relatives or friends

A short family visit uses a different process from moving to the Netherlands. Start with the NetherlandsWorldwide page for the applicant’s country of residence. In Nigeria, the application location depends on the applicant’s state. Ghana and Senegal have their own instructions. [Nigeria](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-nigeria) · [Ghana](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-ghana) · [Senegal](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-senegal) · [Other locations](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/where-to-apply)

- **For the visitor.** Use the current family-and-friends checklist for proof of accommodation, travel medical insurance and the documents that show how you’ll pay and what ties you have to your country of residence. The checklist gives examples for employees, self-employed applicants and students. Describe the purpose and plans for the visit accurately. [Family-and-friends checklist](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/checklist-schengen-visa-family-friends)
- **For the host.** Providing a place to stay and paying for the visit are separate roles. Check which parts of the accommodation and sponsorship form apply and whether the municipality needs to legalise your signature. [Inviting someone for a short stay](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/invite-to-netherlands)

## Settle in and find your community

- **Register and get set up.** Start with registration and your BSN, then use the guides for your DigiD and bank account. [Register and get a BSN](/guides/register-and-get-a-bsn/) · [Apply for DigiD](/guides/digid/) · [Open a bank account](/guides/bank-account/)
- **Practise Dutch.** NT2 TaalMenu has free exercises. If you receive a civic integration letter, follow the requirements DUO gives you for your situation. [NT2 TaalMenu](https://nt2taalmenu.nl/) · [DUO civic integration](https://inburgeren.nl/en/integration-in-the-netherlands/index.jsp)
- **Use the Nigerian community directory.** Naija @ Netherlands collects community guides on housing, the first month as an international student, moving from Nigeria, and a directory of Nigerian businesses. For immigration or document requirements, use the IND and NetherlandsWorldwide links above. [Naija @ Netherlands on Notion](https://naija-netherlands.notion.site/441250b4de41442397dc49bb9da741e6?v=4f1c9b12c1ff48739b0aef7a5c678673)
