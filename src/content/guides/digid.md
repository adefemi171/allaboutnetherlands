DigiD is your personal login for Dutch government and healthcare websites. This guide explains who can get one, how to apply and activate it, how the app works, and what to do while you wait or if you live abroad. It is for anyone with a BSN.

## What DigiD is for

With DigiD you prove who you are online. You use it at the tax office, your municipality, your [health insurer](/guides/health-insurance/), hospitals, pension funds and schools. You need it, for example, to apply for healthcare allowance online.

DigiD is not required by law. Your DigiD is strictly personal, so never share it. If you want someone to arrange things for you, use DigiD Authorisation (*DigiD Machtigen*). That person then logs in with their own DigiD.

## Who can get one

You need three things:

- a citizen service number (BSN)
- an address registered with a Dutch municipality
- a mobile phone

So [register your address and get a BSN](/guides/register-and-get-a-bsn/) first. If you have a BSN but no registered Dutch address, use the route for people abroad at the end of this guide.

Children need their own DigiD. A parent applies for a child under 14. From age 14, children apply themselves.

## Apply and activate

1. Go to digid.nl and fill in the application form. You need your BSN. Choose a username and password, and remember them.
2. Wait for the letter with your activation code. It comes by post within 5 working days. It only goes to the address where you are registered. The Dutch digid.nl page says 5 working days. The English digid.nl page says 3 business days in one place and 5 working days in another, and Government.nl says 3. So count on 5.
3. Download the DigiD app and activate with the code from the letter. This activates your account and the app together.

Three things can go wrong:

- The code is valid for 21 days. After that you must apply again.
- If you type the wrong code 3 times, the application stops and you must apply again.
- You can ask for a replacement letter only once. It also takes up to 5 working days.

## The app and its security levels

There are several ways to log in. They do not all give the same access.

| How you log in | Level | Where it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Username and password only | Basis | Fewer and fewer places. It is being phased out. |
| Password plus a code by text message (SMS) | Midden | Most services. Foreign phone numbers work too. |
| DigiD app with your PIN | Midden | Most services. |
| DigiD app with ID check | Substantieel | Everything, including sensitive data such as health records. |

DigiD has a fourth level, Hoog. It needs an ID check at every login and also only works with a Dutch identity document.

Use the app. You only need to remember a PIN, and you can have it on up to 5 devices.

The ID check is the difficult part for newcomers. You scan an identity document with your phone. It only works with a Dutch passport, identity card or driving licence. A foreign passport or a Dutch residence permit does not work.

What this means for you:

- Skip the ID check when you set up the app. Activate the app with a code by text message instead.
- More and more organisations ask for the ID check. Until 1 July 2028 they may still accept the app without it.
- If an organisation insists on the ID check, contact that organisation and ask for another way. DigiD itself offers no other way for people with a foreign identity document. Logius, which runs DigiD, tells organisations to keep a paper route for people who cannot log in.
- If you later get a Dutch driving licence, add the ID check with that. You may be asked to repeat it once a year.

## While you wait

You can still arrange most things without DigiD. Ask the organisation how. Often you can phone, send a form by post or visit a desk.

If you are from another EU or EEA country, you may be able to log in with the national login of your own country. Look for a button called "European login" on the login page. The tax office, SVB, DUO and UWV offer this. You usually still need a BSN.

Not every country and login method is approved. Netherlands Worldwide keeps a list of approved login methods per country and updates it regularly. On 15 November 2026 it listed Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden. The country overview on Government.nl is shorter. It does not show Cyprus, Finland or Romania. Check the list for your own login method before you rely on it.

## If you live abroad or stay less than 4 months

You can get a DigiD without a Dutch address if you have a BSN and are registered as a non-resident (RNI). The letter is replaced by a check of your identity.

1. Book an appointment at a DigiD service desk before you apply. There are desks at some Dutch embassies and consulates, at some Dutch municipalities and at Schiphol. If you are outside the Netherlands, you can also book a video call with Netherlands Worldwide.
2. Apply on digid.nl. You need your BSN, a valid passport or identity card (not a driving licence), a phone that can receive text messages and an email address.
3. You get a service desk code. It is valid for 30 days.
4. Show your code and your ID at the desk or in the video call. You get an activation code, which is valid for 21 days.

If you register at an RNI desk for a short stay, ask about DigiD too. The RNI desks in Breda, Doetinchem, Heerlen, Nijmegen, The Hague, Venlo and Westland can also activate a DigiD, but you need a separate appointment.

## Keep it working

- Before you leave the Netherlands, set up the app and add your phone number. DigiD letters only go to a registered Dutch address.
- A DigiD that you do not use for 3 years expires.

## Get help

Call the DigiD helpdesk on 088 123 65 55. From abroad, call +31 88 123 65 55. It is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 22:00 and Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00, Dutch time. You can also use the contact form on digid.nl.
